# Office in Copilot goes GA worldwide Word, Excel and PowerPoint editing inside the Microsof

Microsoft's Message Center says the worldwide general availability rollout of Office in Copilot should finish by mid-November 2026. Users can create, edit and co-author Word, Excel and PowerPoint files without leaving the Copilot app, and the files sync back to the normal Office apps. Deferred release starts in early December 2026.

## Details

**Availability and price**
GA worldwide, rollout expected to complete by mid-November 2026; deferred release from early December 2026. No admin action required; subject to existing Microsoft 365 Copilot configuration and policies, managed through Entra ID group-based assignment. The Message Center entry does not state licence requirements or price.

**Caveats**
Microsoft notes Copilot gains expanded ability to read and edit Office document content, which changes how existing data is accessed, so review policies first. The timeline has already shifted once. Microsoft Ignite (17-20 November) may bring further changes.

**Client use cases**
- Make Copilot the single place where staff draft, edit and review reports, decks and models
- Finance teams building Excel models with Copilot's edits shown for review
- Branded PowerPoint production from a prompt, using the client's custom templates
- Cutting app-switching for frontline managers who mostly work in chat

**How we could offer it**
- Adoption sprint: policy review, Entra group-based rollout plan and user comms before the deferred-release wave
- Governance check that sensitivity labels, DLP and sharing rules behave as expected when Copilot edits Office content
- Skills and connector build-out (e.g. finance or legal skills) on top of the new editing surface

**Pitch**
Your people can now go from a Copilot conversation to a finished, governed Word, Excel or PowerPoint file without leaving the app. We make sure the rollout, labels and policies are right before it reaches everyone.

**What it is**
Office in Copilot (MC1530611) puts Word, Excel and PowerPoint inside the Microsoft Copilot app. A user can start a draft in Chat or Cowork, then edit it in the same place, with follow-up prompts that refine the document. Files are saved to OneDrive. Microsoft's launch post says formatting, comments, sharing permissions and version history are kept, existing sensitivity labels and compliance controls carry over, and co-authoring and presence work as in Office. A Library view gathers recent Office files, pages and AI-generated images. Users can pick a model per task or let Auto choose. It launched to Frontier customers on 14 October; the Message Center entry was last updated on 10 November with the GA timeline.
