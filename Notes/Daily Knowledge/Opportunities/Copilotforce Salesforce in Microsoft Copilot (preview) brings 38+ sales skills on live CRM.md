# Copilotforce Salesforce in Microsoft Copilot (preview) brings 38+ sales skills on live CRM

Microsoft and Salesforce launched a Salesforce plugin for Microsoft Copilot in public preview, with 38+ prebuilt sales skills such as Pipeline Review, Deal Review and Call Follow-Up working on live Salesforce records in Copilot Chat and Cowork. Data Federation from OneLake into Salesforce Data 360 is generally available, the Data 360 shortcut into OneLake is in preview, and a Sales Cloud app in Teams is in pilot.

## Details

**Availability and price**
Salesforce in Copilot: public preview (beta on AgentExchange) from 17 Nov. Data Federation into Data 360: GA. Data 360 shortcut into OneLake: preview. Sales Cloud in Teams: pilot, open beta later this year. Needs a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence and a supported Salesforce edition; no pricing given; EU availability not stated.

**Caveats**
Preview only for the plugin. Accenture is the named design and implementation partner and pilot customer, so competitors already have references.

**Client use cases**
- Sales teams on Microsoft 365 Copilot and Salesforce preparing pipeline and deal reviews from email, meetings and CRM in one place
- Account managers drafting follow-ups after calls with CRM context
- Joining Fabric finance or operations data with Salesforce data without copies

**How we could offer it**
- Pilot with one sales team: activate the plugin, map permissions, measure time saved in pipeline reviews
- Data integration project for the OneLake to Data 360 federation with governance in Purview and Fabric
- Change management and skill tailoring for sales processes

**Pitch**
Reps get Salesforce deal data inside the Copilot they already use, with Salesforce permissions respected and no data copied.

**What it is**
A Salesforce plugin for Microsoft Copilot that uses authenticated Salesforce MCP access and the user's existing Salesforce permissions, asking before it changes a record. It ships 38+ prebuilt sales skills, among them Pipeline Review, Close Planning, Deal Review, Customer Health, Renewal Radar and Call Follow-Up. On the data side, Fabric OneLake data can be federated into Salesforce Data 360 (GA) and Data 360 data can be shared into OneLake through a shortcut (preview), giving a two-way zero-copy link.
