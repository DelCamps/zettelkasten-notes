# Microsoft moves data governance into Fabric's OneLake catalog and will retire Purview Data

Microsoft says 'The OneLake catalog is now the single foundation for governing data across your entire estate', while Purview narrows to data security and compliance. Purview Data Governance support ends 12 November 2027, and transition guidance went live on 12 November 2026.

## Details

**Availability and price**
Transition support and guidance available from 12 November 2026; Purview Data Governance support ends 12 November 2027. Governance included in Fabric capacity; no prices given.

**Caveats**
The post gives no step-by-step migration path or GA/preview status for individual governance features; regulated or contract-bound customers are told to engage their account team for case-by-case assessment.

**Client use cases**
- Purview Data Governance customers who must migrate within a year
- Clients preparing data estates for Copilot and Fabric data agents who need trusted, labelled, endorsed data
- Clients using Atlan, Alation, Collibra or Informatica who must decide how those fit with OneLake catalog

**How we could offer it**
- Fixed-scope migration assessment and plan from Purview Data Governance to OneLake catalog
- AI-ready data governance package combining OneLake catalog with Purview labelling and DLP

**Pitch**
Your governance tool is being retired; let's move it once and make the data ready for agents at the same time.

**What it is**
A strategy change announced on the Microsoft Security Community blog: data governance (catalog, lineage, endorsements, business context) moves into Microsoft Fabric via the OneLake catalog, and governance is included in Fabric capacity rather than billed through separate meters. Purview keeps classification, labelling, protection and data loss prevention. Microsoft argues AI agents need to know what data means, who can use it and whether it can be trusted, and that the same permissions, sensitivity labels, endorsements and lineage that govern data also govern how AI uses it.
