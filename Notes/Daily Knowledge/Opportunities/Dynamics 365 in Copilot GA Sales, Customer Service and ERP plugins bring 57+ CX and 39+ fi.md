# Dynamics 365 in Copilot GA Sales, Customer Service and ERP plugins bring 57+ CX and 39+ fi

At Ignite Microsoft made the updated Dynamics 365 Sales, Customer Service and ERP apps plugins generally available in Copilot, and says its own sales team saw a greater than 25% reduction in seller effort on account planning, meeting prep and proposals. Dynamics 365 Activate for CRM (Salesforce to Dynamics 365 migration) is GA, Activate for SAP ECC is in private preview, and Contact Center Intelligence is GA.

## Details

**Availability and price**
GA: Sales plugin, Customer Service plugin, ERP apps plugin, Dynamics 365 Activate for CRM, Contact Center Intelligence, Microsoft Copilot harness in Copilot Studio. Private preview: Activate for ERP (SAP ECC). Agent Accounts public preview in December 2026. The blog gives no prices or licence requirements.

**Caveats**
The 25% effort reduction and 9% faster deal closure come from Microsoft's own internal Customer Zero study. Partner migration timings are quotes, not benchmarks. Licensing for plugins is not stated.

**Client use cases**
- Sellers prepare meetings and account plans from CRM data inside Copilot
- Source-to-pay teams create RFQs and manage POs without leaving Copilot
- Salesforce-to-Dynamics 365 migrations with agentic discovery and data migration (Activate for CRM)

**How we could offer it**
- Copilot for sales and finance pilot with KPI baselines against Microsoft's 25% effort claim
- Migration offering using Activate for CRM, and SAP ECC discovery once preview opens
- Plugin and skill customization, plus Agent 365 cost management and chargeback setup

**Pitch**
Microsoft now ships GA Copilot plugins that put Dynamics 365 sales, service and ERP work inside Copilot, plus agentic Salesforce migration tooling. That gives us a ready-made front-office and finance Copilot offer for Dynamics clients, and a migration play.

**What it is**
Plugins that bring Dynamics 365 context, skills and actions into Microsoft Copilot: account plans, meeting prep, deal insights and PowerPoint deck updates for sellers; service skills; and ERP skills such as requisitions, RFQs and purchase orders with Excel connectivity. Dynamics 365 data is natively in Work IQ, so users can update records or prepare journal entries in plain language under existing permissions.
