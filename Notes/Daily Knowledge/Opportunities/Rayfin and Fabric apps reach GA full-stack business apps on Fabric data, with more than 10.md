# Rayfin and Fabric apps reach GA full-stack business apps on Fabric data, with more than 10

Microsoft's Ignite Book of News says Rayfin and Fabric apps are generally available, giving developers and AI agents databases, authentication, backend services and governed Fabric data for complete business applications. New GA features add central app governance in the OneLake Governance hub, security controls and observability, plus integrations with Microsoft Copilot and Power BI.

## Details

**Availability and price**
Rayfin and Fabric apps: GA per the Book of News. Power BI custom data app creation: preview for Power BI Pro customers in Power BI Desktop. Fabric HorizonDB: preview. No price or regional details in the source.

**Caveats**
Source is the Book of News summary; the Fabric blog post could not be fetched. Microsoft's IQ blog still lists some Fabric apps features (embedding conversational experiences) as preview, so check which parts are GA. Capacity costs not stated.

**Client use cases**
- Turn Power BI semantic models into interactive apps that act, not just report
- Back AI-built Copilot apps with a real database via Copilot Managed Runtime
- Replace spreadsheet-and-email processes with governed data apps on OneLake

**How we could offer it**
- Data-app accelerator on an existing Fabric estate: pick 2-3 analytics-to-action use cases
- Governance setup in the OneLake Governance hub for app sprawl
- Skills: Fabric, Power BI semantic models, SQL; pairs with a Copilot Managed Runtime offer

**Pitch**
Fabric clients can now build governed business apps directly on their trusted data, and Microsoft says over 100,000 were built in preview. We can package a Fabric data-app accelerator next to our Power BI work.

**What it is**
Rayfin is an application backend inside Microsoft Fabric. Developers pick Microsoft SQL database or Fabric HorizonDB, connect to existing semantic models, warehouses and lakehouses, and reuse trusted business definitions and access controls without building a separate backend stack. Builders created more than 100,000 apps with Rayfin since its preview.
