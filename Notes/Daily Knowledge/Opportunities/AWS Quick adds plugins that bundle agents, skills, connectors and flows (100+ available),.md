# AWS Quick adds plugins that bundle agents, skills, connectors and flows (100+ available),

AWS Quick now installs plugins that package the connectors, skills, agents and flows a job needs in one step. AWS has official plugins such as Sales Development, and builders can create custom ones by describing the job in chat. Separately, a new Quick web app syncs with desktop and mobile, keeps scheduled agents running after the browser closes, adds a Mission Control view, and updates the Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook add-ins.

## Details

**Availability and price**
Plugins are available in all Regions where Quick is offered. The web experience is available in US East (N. Virginia), US West (Oregon), Europe (Frankfurt), Europe (Ireland), Europe (London), Asia Pacific (Sydney) and Asia Pacific (Tokyo). The mobile app is free. The posts give no price or GA/preview label for plugins.

**Caveats**
The announcement says little about how plugins are governed or versioned. Enterprise guardrails are still in preview. Clients standardised on Microsoft 365 Copilot may see overlap.

**Client use cases**
- Role packs, for example a finance close or legal review plugin, rolled out to a department in one install
- AWS-centric clients that want Copilot-style help inside Microsoft 365 apps through the Quick add-ins
- Accounts-payable flows: agents read invoices and deterministic steps check totals and duplicates

**How we could offer it**
- Build industry or role plugins as reusable PwC assets for AWS-first clients
- Pilot one department's plugin, with guardrails (preview) and group-based limit profiles
- Governance review of custom-plugin sharing and connector scopes

**Pitch**
Quick plugins let us package a client workflow once and deploy it to a whole team, much like Copilot plugins. That gives us a repeatable offering for AWS-first clients.

**What it is**
Plugins in AWS Quick, AWS's enterprise agent workspace, are installable bundles for a task, role or industry. More than 100 skills and plugins cover productivity, finance, marketing, legal, industries and business apps. Custom plugins are assembled from a chat description and shared with teammates. The new web experience gives the browser the desktop's agents, skills and connectors. It adds a daily briefing and a prioritised email and message feed, and lets agents keep running after the browser closes. Mission Control shows what agents have finished and where they need input. Flows, updated a day earlier, now add deterministic steps and validations.
