# Salesforce Agentforce Coworker adds Microsoft 365 connectivity, Skills and a $50 per-user

Salesforce says Agentforce Coworker usage grew 21x in three months to more than 185,000 users. It made Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace connectivity, Skills, Conversation History and a $50 per-user-per-month option available now. Coworker in Microsoft Teams, desktop and iOS apps and a Coworker MCP server are slated for GA in November 2026.

## Details

**Availability and price**
Available now: Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace connectivity, Conversation History, Skills and a $50 per user per month option. GA in November 2026: Teams app, iOS app, Mac and Windows desktop app, Coworker MCP server, and a free trial for Flex Credits customers. Expected December 2026: Daily Digest and scheduled tasks.

**Caveats**
The Teams app and MCP server are not GA yet. Salesforce says pricing and packaging may change and that buyers should decide based on products that are already released.

**Client use cases**
- Meeting briefs and renewal plans that combine CRM records with Outlook mail and calendar context
- Turning a top rep's renewal playbook into a shared Skill for the whole sales team
- Sales teams working from Teams once the Teams app is GA

**How we could offer it**
- Coworker pilots for Salesforce-plus-Microsoft 365 clients, with Skill design for sales and service playbooks
- Positioning against or alongside Microsoft Copilot for Sales in M365 tenants

**Pitch**
Give your sellers one agent that knows both the CRM and their inbox, priced per user so it is easy to budget.

**What it is**
Agentforce Coworker is Salesforce's CRM-grounded work agent. It now combines each account's email, calendar and files from Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace with Salesforce data. Skills save a proven workflow so anyone can run it with a short request, and Conversation History lets users resume work across Salesforce, Teams and the new apps.
