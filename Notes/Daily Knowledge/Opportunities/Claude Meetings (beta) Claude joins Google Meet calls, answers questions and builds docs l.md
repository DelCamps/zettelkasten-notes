# Claude Meetings (beta) Claude joins Google Meet calls, answers questions and builds docs l

Claude can join video calls as a visible participant, answer spoken or chat questions, use connected tools with permission, build docs and whiteboards during the call, and write notes and action items. The beta is rolling out to Team and Enterprise plans on Google Meet only, with other platforms coming soon; notes and transcripts are free, and artifacts made in calls are free through February 10, 2027.

## Details

**Availability and price**
Beta, Team and Enterprise plans. Google Meet now; other platforms coming soon. Notes, transcripts and in-call answers free (connected-tool use counts toward plan usage). Not available in beta for ZDR, HIPAA-ready or CMEK organizations.

**Caveats**
No Microsoft Teams support yet, which limits most PwC clients; by default Claude stays out of calls with outside invitees.

**Client use cases**
- Workshops where Claude drafts the requirements doc as people talk
- Steering committees with automatic action items and follow-up tasks in connected tools
- Sales calls feeding Gong, Granola, Otter or Fireflies.ai (transcript sending coming soon)

**How we could offer it**
- Assessment: consent, transcription and works-council implications before enabling auto-join
- Pilot with a Google Workspace client's internal meetings
- Governance: admin policy for calls with external guests

**Pitch**
Claude Meetings turns client meetings into finished drafts and tasks. We can pilot it with a consent and governance pack for EU clients.

**What it is**
A meeting agent that turns calls into work: each meeting gets a page in claude.ai with notes, transcript and anything created. It saves transcripts, summaries and screen descriptions but not audio.
