# Amazon Quick Essentials Quick Sight Reader users upgraded to AI chat, Research, Flows and

AWS has launched Quick Essentials, a new Amazon Quick enterprise plan that bundles AI chat, Research, Flows and no-code Apps with Quick Sight dashboards. Quick Sight Reader users are upgraded to it 'at no additional per-user cost' in regions where Quick is available.

## Details

**Availability and price**
Phased rollout in AWS Regions where Quick AI features are available. Quick Sight Reader users are upgraded at no additional per-user cost; for other pricing, see the Quick pricing page.

**Caveats**
Phased rollout, and the announcement does not name the regions. AWS says row- and column-level security apply to every answer, but clients should test this on their own data.

**Client use cases**
- Turning existing Quick Sight reader estates into AI-assisted analytics with no new licence spend
- Scheduled management updates through Flows
- Self-service research reports with citations for business teams

**How we could offer it**
- An adoption and enablement programme for clients with large Quick Sight Reader bases
- Governance setup: permission profiles plus checks on row- and column-level security

**Pitch**
You already pay for Quick Sight readers, and they now get AI chat, research and automation for free. We help you switch it on safely and get real usage.

**What it is**
An entry plan for Amazon Quick. Users can ask plain-language questions about their dashboards and turn the answers into documents, presentations or spreadsheets. Research produces cited reports from data, files and the web, Flows sends recurring updates, and Apps builds custom tools from a description. Admins control features through custom permission profiles.
