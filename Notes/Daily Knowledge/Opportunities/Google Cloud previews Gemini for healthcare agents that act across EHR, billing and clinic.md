# Google Cloud previews Gemini for healthcare agents that act across EHR, billing and clinic

Google Cloud launched Gemini for healthcare in preview, with skills and data connectors to EHR, billing platforms, clinical guidelines and Medicare datasets, and multi-step workflows such as prior authorization review. Google cites clinicians losing nearly 28 hours a week to paperwork; launch partners are HCA Healthcare, Highmark Health and Northwell Health.

## Details

**Availability and price**
Preview, with launch partners HCA Healthcare, Highmark Health and Northwell Health. The post gives no pricing, licensing, GA date or EU availability.

**Caveats**
Preview only, and launch partners are US health systems; Medicare datasets are US-specific. EU and Spanish availability and data residency are unconfirmed.

**Client use cases**
- Prior authorization review for payers and providers
- Medical records synthesis before consultations
- Administrative workload reduction for clinical staff
- Linking billing and clinical data for revenue-cycle work

**How we could offer it**
- Use-case and data-readiness assessment for hospital and insurer clients
- Pilot on one workflow (e.g. prior authorization) with human-oversight design and clinical safety review
- Regulatory and governance work: health-data protection, AI Act and audit trails

**Pitch**
Google now offers a healthcare agent platform that works across EHR, billing and guidelines instead of one more chatbot. We can pilot one high-cost administrative workflow and build the oversight regulators will expect.

**What it is**
Google calls it "an AI solution engineered to unify fragmented clinical, operational, and administrative systems." It connects specialized skills and data connectors to existing third-party systems, drawing on electronic health records, billing platforms, clinical guidelines and Medicare datasets. It orchestrates multi-step workflows, such as medical records synthesis and prior authorization review, within defined guardrails and with human oversight. Google frames it as going beyond information retrieval to acting on siloed data, and cites a 2024 Google Cloud and Harris Poll finding that clinicians lose nearly 28 hours each week to paperwork and administrative tasks.
