# Copilot in Excel gets accounting skills (Frontier) reconciliations, journal entries and va

Microsoft's November Excel update adds accounting skills to Copilot in Excel and Cowork through the Frontier program: Copilot can reconcile accounts and prepare reconciling items, draft journal entries and check that they balance, and write plan-versus-actual variance reports. Copilot also now works in read-only workbooks, and a 'Fix this error' button repairs supported formula errors in Excel for Windows.

## Details

**Availability and price**
Accounting skills: Frontier program only, in Copilot in Excel and Cowork. Copilot in read-only workbooks: rolling out on Windows, Mac and web. Fix this error: rolling out to Excel for Windows Version 2611 or later. Extra models (GPT-6.2 Astra, Claude Fable 5.2 and others) appear only with usage-based billing and allocated credits.

**Caveats**
Frontier is an early-access programme, not GA. The post gives no accuracy figures, and outputs still need human review.

**Client use cases**
- Month-end close: bank and intercompany reconciliations with reconciling items for review
- Drafting and balance-checking journal entries
- Plan-versus-actual variance commentary for management reporting

**How we could offer it**
- Frontier pilot with a client finance team, measuring hours saved on close
- Controls design: reviewer sign-off on Copilot-prepared entries and reconciliations

**Pitch**
Copilot now does the first draft of the month-end close inside Excel, which finance teams already use. We can pilot it with review controls an auditor would accept.

**What it is**
Finance skills for month-end close inside Copilot in Excel and Copilot Cowork. Copilot compares balances and transactions to reconcile accounts, drafts several types of journal entries, verifies that required inputs are present and entries balance, and produces variance reports explaining plan versus actual. The same update brings Copilot chat to read-only workbooks, Copilot repair for formula errors, and GPT-6.2 Sol in the Excel model picker.
