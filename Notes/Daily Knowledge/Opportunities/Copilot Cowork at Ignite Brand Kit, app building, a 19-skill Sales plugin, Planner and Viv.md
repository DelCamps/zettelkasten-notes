# Copilot Cowork at Ignite Brand Kit, app building, a 19-skill Sales plugin, Planner and Viv

Microsoft says nearly 80% of the Fortune 500 now use Copilot Cowork. At Ignite it made several features generally available: Brand Kit (on-brand decks and documents from the first draft), app building, Sales, Planner and Viva Engage plugins, and Cowork in Edge for Business for multi-step web tasks. Auto with subagents, proactive recommendations, a Workforce Intelligence plugin and Agent 365 Business Analytics went to Frontier (preview) only.

## Details

**Availability and price**
Brand Kit, app building, the Sales, Planner and Viva Engage plugins, and Cowork in Edge for Business are GA (Brand Kit is 'rolling out now'). Auto subagents, proactive recommendations, Workforce Intelligence and Agent 365 Business Analytics are Frontier only. Cowork in GCC starts rolling out this month. The post gives no price or EU details.

**Caveats**
Microsoft's adoption figure is self-reported. Brand Kit is still rolling out. The analytics that would prove ROI are not GA. Browser automation needs clear approval rules and data-loss-prevention (DLP) review.

**Client use cases**
- Sales teams: quarterly business review decks and proposal responses from CRM and Microsoft 365 data
- Marketing and communications: on-brand first drafts without restyling
- Operations: turning a weekly tracker into a governed Cowork app
- Back-office web tasks across portals that have no API

**How we could offer it**
- Cowork adoption sprint: set up brand kits, choose plugins and skills in the admin catalog, and train champions
- Governance: rules for which apps go on the Managed Runtime, approval policies for browser tasks, and Agent 365 cost limits
- Use-case value tracking, later using Agent 365 Business Analytics once it leaves Frontier

**Pitch**
Cowork now ships the pieces that make it useful for sales and marketing teams: brand-safe output, a 19-skill sales toolkit and governed apps. We can package a short Cowork rollout for clients that already have Microsoft 365 Copilot licences.

**What it is**
Cowork is Microsoft 365 Copilot's agent for delegating multi-step work. The GA set: Brand Kit applies a named brand kit's colours, fonts and voice. App building turns a task result into a shareable app that runs on the Copilot Managed Runtime. The Sales plugin adds 19 skills for account briefs, pitch decks, business reviews, proposal responses and pipeline insights from any CRM. Planner and Viva Engage plugins are also GA. Cowork in Edge for Business does web tasks from the side pane, works only on sites the user can already access, and asks for approval before sensitive actions.
