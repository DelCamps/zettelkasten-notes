# ChatGPT Enterprise gets dots, always-on personal agents that keep working between conversa

OpenAI made dots generally available to eligible ChatGPT Enterprise, Edu and Healthcare workspaces. Dots are persistent personal agents that keep making progress on tasks between conversations. They are in admin preview until 18 November and then on by default, and they support data residency, Enterprise Key Management and HIPAA eligibility.

## Details

**Availability and price**
Generally available to eligible Enterprise, Edu and Healthcare workspaces. Admin preview until 18 November 2026, then available by default. Supports data residency and Enterprise Key Management, and is HIPAA-eligible in covered workspaces. No price is given in the release note.

**Caveats**
Our check relies on the release-note text as mirrored by releases.sh, because help.openai.com blocked direct fetching. Usage terms per plan are not stated in that note. This is mainly relevant for clients on ChatGPT Enterprise rather than Microsoft 365 Copilot.

**Client use cases**
- Recurring monitoring and briefing tasks for knowledge workers, such as watchlists or weekly status digests
- Long-running research or follow-up tasks that span days
- Healthcare and regulated clients that need EKM and data residency for agent use

**How we could offer it**
- Before 18 November, help ChatGPT Enterprise clients decide on access policies and connections for dots
- Run use-case design and governance sessions for persistent agents, with usage review

**Pitch**
ChatGPT Enterprise clients get persistent agents switched on by default after 18 November. We can help them set access rules and pick the first recurring workflows before that happens.

**What it is**
Dots are always-on personal agents in ChatGPT that take ongoing responsibility for a task and keep working between conversations, with scheduled work and reminders. In Enterprise, Edu and Healthcare workspaces, owners and admins can control which members use them, see personal dots and their connections, pause or resume them, review usage and admin activity, and delete personal dots.
