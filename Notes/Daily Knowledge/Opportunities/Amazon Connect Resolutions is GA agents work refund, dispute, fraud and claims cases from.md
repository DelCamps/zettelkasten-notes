# Amazon Connect Resolutions is GA agents work refund, dispute, fraud and claims cases from

AWS made Resolutions generally available in Amazon Connect Customer. Managers describe a process or upload SOPs, Resolutions builds a work plan, opens a case for each request and uses AI agents to gather data and act across business apps, while specialists handle approvals and exceptions and every action is logged.

## Details

**Availability and price**
GA, announced 19 November 2026. Regions and pricing are not stated in the announcement (it links to the Connect Customer pricing page).

**Caveats**
Requires Amazon Connect Customer; EU region availability unconfirmed. Results depend on how well SOPs and exceptions are defined.

**Client use cases**
- Card transaction disputes in banking, with specialists reviewing only cases that need judgment
- Refund requests in retail and telco
- Fraud investigations and insurance claims handling

**How we could offer it**
- Process assessment: turn a client's SOPs into decision rules and approval thresholds
- Pilot on one high-volume queue, measuring cycle time and exception rate
- Governance and managed operations, including audit review of agent decisions

**Pitch**
This turns our process and controls work into a running agentic operation for back-office queues, on AWS. It fits clients already on Amazon Connect who want automation with a clear audit trail.

**What it is**
An agentic operations product for high-volume case work that combines AI agent reasoning with deterministic workflows. It asks clarifying questions to set exceptions, decision rules and approval thresholds, then applies the plan to each request and gives an auditable, real-time view of progress and service-level risk.
