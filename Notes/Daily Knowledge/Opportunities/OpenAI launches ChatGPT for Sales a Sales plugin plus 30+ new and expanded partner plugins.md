# OpenAI launches ChatGPT for Sales a Sales plugin plus 30+ new and expanded partner plugins

OpenAI introduced ChatGPT for Sales: an OpenAI Sales plugin with dedicated seller skills and more than 30 new or enhanced partner plugins. They cover CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot), prospecting (Apollo, Clay), call notes (Gong, Otter.ai) and quotes (Docusign, ServiceNow CPQ). Business and Enterprise admins enable it from the Admin Console, and 'dots', ChatGPT's always-on agents, can watch deals and prepare briefs.

## Details

**Availability and price**
Admins of ChatGPT Business and Enterprise can enable it under Admin Console > Plugins. Availability of partner plugins and features varies by plan, region and workspace settings. The post gives no price.

**Caveats**
The customer quotes are vendor-selected, and one figure is the customer's own estimate (NeoBid: about 65% less manual effort). Each partner plugin brings its own data-handling terms. The offering overlaps with Microsoft's Sales plugin for Copilot.

**Client use cases**
- Account prioritisation from CRM plus web research
- Pre-meeting briefs and customer-specific pitch decks
- Post-call follow-ups and CRM updates, reviewed before saving
- Pipeline-risk reviews from CRM history and call notes

**How we could offer it**
- Sales-AI assessment comparing ChatGPT for Sales with Microsoft Copilot's Sales plugin and Agentforce, based on the client's CRM and licences
- Pilot with one sales team: choose plugins, set admin defaults, and define write-back approval rules
- Governance: CRM write scopes, data residency of partner plugins, measuring dots' work

**Pitch**
OpenAI now has a packaged sales offering, so for clients on ChatGPT Enterprise we can run a sales-productivity pilot in weeks rather than build custom agents.

**What it is**
A packaged sales experience in ChatGPT. The Sales plugin has ready-made workflows for choosing accounts, preparing calls and drafting follow-ups. Partner plugins bring in CRM context (Salesforce, HubSpot, Close, monday CRM), account signals (Apollo, Clay, Common Room), outreach (Outreach, Salesloft), call notes (Gong, Otter.ai, Granola), deal risk (Backstory, Rox) and quotes (Docusign, Ironclad, PandaDoc, ServiceNow CPQ). Dots can check a deal each morning and propose next steps for review.
