# Microsoft Copilot for finance accounting skills, Capital Markets and Finance plugins let f

Microsoft launched Copilot for finance on 29 October, adding more than 25 new finance skills and partner plugins across Copilot in Excel and Copilot Cowork, plus a Dynamics 365 ERP plugin and Fabric IQ grounding on Power BI semantic models. The Capital Markets and Finance plugins in Cowork and the finance skills in Excel are generally available; accounting skills are in Frontier. For PwC this lines up directly with our finance-transformation, audit and deals work on a stack clients already license.

## Details

**What it is**
A role-specific package for finance built on Microsoft 365 Copilot. Excel now sits inside the Copilot app, so Chat or Cowork can analyse, edit and co-author workbooks. New pieces: accounting capabilities (account reconciliation, journal-entry preparation, variance-to-plan reporting, with an optional audit trail of what Copilot changed and why); a Capital Markets plugin (investment cases, comparables, LBO, DCF, merger models, CIM review, keeping sourced inputs separate from analyst assumptions); a Finance plugin (close review packages, forecast reviews, audit evidence packages, expense itemisation); Excel history that shows who changed what and can reverse single edits; a Dynamics 365 ERP apps plugin; Fabric IQ for governed Power BI data; and partner plugins (e.g. Moody's, S&P Global, FactSet, LSEG, Fitch, D&B, Xero, QuickBooks). In limited Insiders availability over seven days, more than 8,000 users invoked accounting skills more than 44,000 times across more than 18,000 conversations.

**Client use cases**
- Month-end close: Cowork assembles the close review package from the ERP, the close calendar and the reconciliation workbooks
- Reconciliations and journal entries prepared from Dynamics 365 data, with the Excel audit trail kept as review evidence
- Deals teams: comps, LBO and DCF models plus an investment committee memo drafted with market data from licensed partner plugins
- FP&A: variance-to-plan commentary grounded in the client's Power BI semantic models through Fabric IQ

**How we could offer it**
- Finance Copilot pilot: pick two or three close or FP&A processes, measure hours saved, and set up review controls (audit trail, Excel history) that our assurance people can sign off
- Governance and controls design: who can use which skills, plugin and connector enablement, data-source permissions and evidence retention; needs Finance, Risk and M365 admin skills
- Custom finance skills and plugins for client-specific charts of accounts or non-Microsoft ERPs, built by our AI engineers

**Pitch**
Microsoft just shipped Copilot for finance: GA plugins in Cowork for close packages, audit evidence and deal models, plus Excel skills with an audit trail. That puts finance-transformation and audit-readiness pilots on a Microsoft 365 licence clients already own, and I'd like to put together a 2-3 process pilot offer we can take to our finance clients.

**Availability and price**
Finance skills in Copilot in Excel, and the Capital Markets and Finance plugins in Cowork: generally available. Accounting capabilities: available in Frontier. Fabric IQ: generally available in Cowork. Dynamics 365 ERP plugin and partner plugins: beginning to roll out through the Microsoft plugin registry. The post gives no pricing, licensing or regional details. Cowork itself requires a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence (per Microsoft's June GA post).

**Caveats**
Accounting skills are Frontier-only, which means early-access tenants. The ERP connector is Dynamics 365 only, and the post names no SAP or Oracle plugin, which matters for many PwC clients. Third-party plugins may need separate subscriptions and admin enablement. Our independence rules apply to using these tools on audit clients. Competitors: Claude for Excel and Financial Services, ChatGPT Enterprise, and SAP Joule finance assistants.
