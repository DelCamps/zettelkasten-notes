# Claude for Manufacturing a plugin with 17 skills and connectors to Autodesk Fusion, Infor,

Anthropic launched Claude for Manufacturing, a plugin with 17 skills and four connectors (Autodesk Fusion, Infor, Microsoft 365, Oracle NetSuite) that drafts quotes, corrective action reports, supplier quote requests and production reports, each ending in human review. Infor is releasing its own plugin that lets Claude release purchase orders or reschedule production after user approval; it is available on Team and Enterprise plans.

## Details

**Availability and price**
Team and Enterprise plans. Setup by installing the plugin and asking Claude to set up. No price stated; technical webinar on November 17.

**Caveats**
It does not operate equipment; SAP is not among the connectors; Claude's Infor access follows the user's permissions.

**Client use cases**
- Faster quote turnaround for job shops
- Root-cause analysis and corrective action reports after quality failures
- Supplier RFQ preparation and bid comparison
- Daily production and downtime cost reports

**How we could offer it**
- Assessment of a manufacturer's ERP/MES landscape against the connectors
- Pilot on quoting or quality, with custom skills built on Anthropic's
- Implementation of Infor write actions with approval controls

**Pitch**
Anthropic now ships a manufacturing plugin that covers quoting, quality and sourcing. We can adapt its skills to our industrial clients' systems in a short pilot.

**What it is**
An industry plugin for Claude: skills for quoting a job, investigating a failed inspection, diagnosing a breakdown, re-sourcing from another supplier, comparing designs in Autodesk Fusion and production reporting. Systems without a connector can be used via exports saved to Microsoft 365.
