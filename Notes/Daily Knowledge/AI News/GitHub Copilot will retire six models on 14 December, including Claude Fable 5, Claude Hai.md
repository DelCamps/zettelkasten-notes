# GitHub Copilot will retire six models on 14 December, including Claude Fable 5, Claude Hai

GitHub will deprecate Claude Fable 5, Claude Haiku 4.5, Gemini 3.8 Flash, Gemini 4 Argon, Grok 4.6 and Kimi K3 across all Copilot experiences on December 14, 2026. Suggested replacements (Fable 5.2, Haiku 5.5, Gemini 4 Hydrogen, Gemini 4 Carbon, Grok 4.7) are enabled by default for Business and Enterprise unless admins turned the global default off.

## Details

**Caveats**
Kimi K3 has no named successor yet, so open-weight model users have a gap. Replacement models may cost differently under usage-based billing.

**For your work**
Check client Copilot policies and any agents or scripts that name these models; switch Fable 5 and Haiku 4.5 workflows to Fable 5.2 and Haiku 5.5 and re-run evals before 14 December.

**Method**
- Deprecation covers Copilot Chat, inline edits, ask and agent modes, and code completions
- Replacement mapping: Claude Fable 5 to Fable 5.2; Haiku 4.5 to Haiku 5.5; Gemini 3.8 Flash to Gemini 4 Hydrogen; Gemini 4 Argon to Gemini 4 Carbon; Grok 4.6 to Grok 4.7; Kimi K3 replacement to be announced
- Under default model enablement, alternatives switch on automatically for Copilot Business and Enterprise; if the global default is off, admins enable them through model policies

**Problem**
Teams that pin Copilot models in workflows, custom agents or code-review settings need to know which models disappear and what replaces them.

**Results**
- All six models go on 2026-12-14; no action is needed to remove them
- Claude Haiku 4.5 stays for individual subscribers on annual plans
- Enterprise customers are told to contact their account team with concerns
