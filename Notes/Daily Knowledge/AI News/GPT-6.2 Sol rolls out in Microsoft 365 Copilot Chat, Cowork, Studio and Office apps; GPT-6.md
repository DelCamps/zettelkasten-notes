# GPT-6.2 Sol rolls out in Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat, Cowork, Studio and Office apps; GPT-6

Microsoft says GPT-6.2 Sol is rolling out worldwide to eligible users in Copilot Chat, Copilot Cowork, Copilot Studio and Copilot in Excel, PowerPoint and Word. GPT-6.2 Astra is rolling out in Copilot Cowork and Copilot Studio, and Frontier users can also use it in Copilot Chat and the Office apps. Both are grounded through Work IQ, which Microsoft says only draws on content each person can already access.

## Details

**Caveats**
Microsoft says availability may vary by region and organization; the post gives no EU details, licence tiers, limits or default-model changes.

**For your work**
Copilot Studio agents built for clients can now be tested on GPT-6.2 Sol and Astra. Re-run your client agent evals before switching models, and check the Copilot roadmap for EU tenant timing.

**Method**
- Rollout covers Copilot Chat, Cowork, Studio and Copilot in Excel, PowerPoint and Word.
- Astra in Chat and the Office apps is limited to Frontier users.
- Admins manage access through the Microsoft 365 admin center.

**Problem**
Clients on Microsoft 365 Copilot want to know when OpenAI's newest models, released on 17 November, reach the tools their staff already use.

**Results**
- GPT-6.2 Sol: rolling out worldwide to eligible users across all listed Copilot surfaces.
- GPT-6.2 Astra: Cowork and Studio for eligible users; Chat and Office apps for Frontier users.
