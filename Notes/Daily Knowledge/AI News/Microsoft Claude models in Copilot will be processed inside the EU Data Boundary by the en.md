# Microsoft Claude models in Copilot will be processed inside the EU Data Boundary by the en

Microsoft says that by the end of 2026, EU and EFTA organizations should be able to use Claude models across Copilot experiences, including Cowork, with processing inside the EU Data Boundary. The Claude setting stays off by default in the EU and EFTA; tenants already using Claude in Copilot will move into the EUDB automatically.

## Details

**Caveats**
An announced target, not yet available; Microsoft will share details closer to availability. No specific Claude model versions are named.

**For your work**
This removes a common blocker for Spanish and EU clients who wanted Claude inside Copilot (Cowork, Code) but whose DPOs required EUDB processing; worth flagging in current Copilot proposals and preparing admin-enablement guidance.

**Method**
- Claude models join the Microsoft-operated models that already process data within the EUDB, Microsoft's commitment to store and process Microsoft 365 data in the EU and EFTA
- The Claude setting remains disabled by default in the EU and EFTA; admins can enable it for all users or selected users and groups in the Microsoft 365 admin center
- Covered by Microsoft enterprise data protection; customer data not used to train foundation models

**Problem**
Claude models already power Copilot Chat, Copilot in Microsoft 365 apps, Copilot Cowork and Copilot Code, but EU customers had to choose between Claude and Microsoft's EU data-processing commitments.

**Results**
- Target: by the end of 2026 for EU and EFTA organizations
- No action needed for tenants already using Claude in Copilot: processing moves automatically
- EUDB is included in contract terms for eligible Microsoft 365 customers, no extra purchase; in-country data residency is offered in 10 countries in the EU and EFTA
