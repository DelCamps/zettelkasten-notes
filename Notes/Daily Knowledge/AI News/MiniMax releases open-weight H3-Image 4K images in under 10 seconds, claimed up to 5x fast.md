# MiniMax releases open-weight H3-Image 4K images in under 10 seconds, claimed up to 5x fast

MiniMax released H3-Image, an open-weight image generation and editing model built on H3, with weights on Hugging Face. It claims a 4096x4096 image in under 10 seconds, up to 5x the speed of mainstream alternatives, and API pricing at 50-70% of comparable offerings.

## Details

**Caveats**
No parameter count, licence or benchmark numbers in the post; speed comparisons are vendor claims against unnamed baselines.

**For your work**
A candidate for self-hosted marketing or document imagery where data cannot leave the client, once the licence terms are checked.

**Method**
- Image generation and editing model built on MiniMax's H3
- Weights published at huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3-Image; also offered through MiniMax's API
- Standard and Highspeed modes

**Problem**
High-resolution image generation is slow and costly, and strong open-weight options for self-hosting are limited.

**Results**
- 4K (4096x4096) image in under 10 seconds
- Standard mode 1.5-2x and Highspeed mode 3-4x as fast as mainstream alternatives (up to 5x)
- API pricing 50-70% of comparable offerings, with 50% off 4K-and-above requests in launch week
- Claims 'state-of-the-art prompt adherence among open-weight models' but gives no scores
