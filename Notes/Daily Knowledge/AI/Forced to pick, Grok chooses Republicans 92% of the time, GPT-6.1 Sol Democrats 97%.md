# Forced to pick, Grok chooses Republicans 92% of the time, GPT-6.1 Sol Democrats 97%

Convovo (Cornell's Tapan Parikh) asked the APIs of Grok 4.7 and GPT-6.1 Sol to pick a candidate in nearly 500 US races; they agreed under 10% of the time, and Grok leaned on X (41%) and partisan voter guides for sources. With search off, GPT still picked the Democrat in 24 of 30 while Grok declined in 27 of 30, and each Grok call carried about 1,240 hidden input tokens. A reminder that model choice silently injects values into downstream answers.
