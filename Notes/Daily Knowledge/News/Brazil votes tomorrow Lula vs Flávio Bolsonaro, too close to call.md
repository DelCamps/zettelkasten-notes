# Brazil votes tomorrow Lula vs Flávio Bolsonaro, too close to call

Sunday's presidential runoff pits 80-year-old Lula against senator Flávio Bolsonaro, who led the first round 47% to 45%; the latest polls (Quaest 45-44, AtlasIntel 49.8-47.6) are within the margin of error. Crime, household debt, Bolsonaro's plan to 'redemocratise' Brazil through constitutional reform and the Vorcaro banking scandal dominate; his father Jair is serving 27 years for the coup plot.
