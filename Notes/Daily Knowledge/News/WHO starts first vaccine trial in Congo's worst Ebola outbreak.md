# WHO starts first vaccine trial in Congo's worst Ebola outbreak

The Bundibugyo-strain outbreak declared in May has passed 9,700 cases and 4,600 deaths and spread to four African countries; it is a WHO international health emergency, and Kenya has reported a second death. Ring vaccination with Ervebo began Friday in Bunia to test cross-protection, with Oxford ChAdOx1 and Moderna mRNA vaccines specific to Bundibugyo due to join the trial shortly.
