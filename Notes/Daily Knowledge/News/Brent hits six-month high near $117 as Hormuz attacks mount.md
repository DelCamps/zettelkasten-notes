# Brent hits six-month high near $117 as Hormuz attacks mount

Brent settled at $116.72 on Friday and WTI rose about 3.8% to just above $103 as October became the worst month for shipping attacks in the strait; Iran says it stays closed until its conditions are met. The Dow fell 1.2% and the S&P 500 0.5%, with selling spreading beyond AI stocks. Expect fuel prices to keep climbing.
