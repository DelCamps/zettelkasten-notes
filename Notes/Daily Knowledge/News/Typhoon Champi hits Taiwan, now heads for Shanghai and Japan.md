# Typhoon Champi hits Taiwan, now heads for Shanghai and Japan

The storm, briefly a super typhoon with winds near 300 km/h, swept past north-east Taiwan on Friday: one dead, more than 500 injured, 5,000 evacuated and 650 mm of rain in Yilan. Shanghai has evacuated tens of thousands and suspended some trains as Champi moves toward China's east coast and then mainland Japan.
