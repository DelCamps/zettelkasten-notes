# Sánchez goes on La Revuelta, and skips Jordi Wild

Pedro Sánchez sat down with David Broncano on TVE's La Revuelta on Thursday night, five weeks before the 29-N election. Asked the show's classic questions, he dodged the money one and answered the sex one with "tenemos una vida sexual activa y sana", which is the clip everyone is sharing. He still hasn't answered Jordi Wild's invitation to The Wild Project, which Feijóo, Abascal and Colau have accepted; Wild's deadline is today.
