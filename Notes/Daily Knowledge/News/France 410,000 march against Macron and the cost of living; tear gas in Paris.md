# France 410,000 march against Macron and the cost of living; tear gas in Paris

The Interior Ministry counted 410,000 protesters nationwide (CGT: 800,000) as the movement that began with school blockades in September broadened into anger at prices and inequality. More than 70,000 police were deployed, the central Paris route was banned, the Louvre and Orsay closed, and at least 135 people were arrested; Macron's Friday meeting with students and promise of more teachers and a Parcoursup overhaul did not calm things, and the Yellow Vests have called another day for 31 October.
