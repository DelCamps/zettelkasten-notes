# Israel locks down the West Bank and launches a multi-day Hebron operation

After two Palestinian gunmen killed two Israeli reservists near the Telem settlement, Netanyahu ordered a full West Bank lockdown, with nearly 1,000 checkpoints and gates closed. Troops raided at least 15 towns in the north and began a Hebron operation set to last several days; Hebron's mayor says about 2,200 people have been displaced, and Ben-Gvir called for full Israeli control of the territory.
