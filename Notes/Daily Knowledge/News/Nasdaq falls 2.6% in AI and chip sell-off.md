# Nasdaq falls 2.6% in AI and chip sell-off

Worst day since June, wiping about $1.3T from the Nasdaq-100; Nvidia alone lost about $235B. The triggers were Meta's ~$810B in data-centre lease commitments, a supply warning from Lam Research, and Brent near $113 on the Iran war. KOSPI opened down more than 3%.
