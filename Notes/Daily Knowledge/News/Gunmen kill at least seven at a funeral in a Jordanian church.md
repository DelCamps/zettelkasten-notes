# Gunmen kill at least seven at a funeral in a Jordanian church

Two masked attackers opened fire during a funeral at a Greek Orthodox church in Hamoud, Karak governorate, on Friday, killing at least seven and wounding 34. No group has claimed it; the government calls it terrorism and King Abdullah promised the harshest penalties.
