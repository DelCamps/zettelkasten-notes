# Sánchez says yes to Jordi Wild after record La Revuelta night

On the last day of the deadline Sánchez replied on X, 'Gracias por la invitación, @JordiWild. Ahí estaré', so The Wild Project now has all four candidates (Colau, Feijóo, Abascal, Sánchez); the interviews go out on 13 November, first day of the campaign, with off-camera fact-checkers. His La Revuelta appearance drew 4.4 million viewers (33.9% share), Broncano's all-time record.
