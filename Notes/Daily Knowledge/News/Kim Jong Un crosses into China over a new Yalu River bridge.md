# Kim Jong Un crosses into China over a new Yalu River bridge

North Korea's leader made his first trip to China in about a year, entering Dandong over the newly opened New Yalu River Bridge. He met senior party official Wang Huning rather than Xi Jinping and marked 76 years since China entered the Korean War; analysts see the bridge as a big boost to China-North Korea trade.
